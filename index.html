About Me

Academic Excellence

Leadership

I have served in various capacities including Principal, Director-Research, and HOD across prestigious institutions in Karnataka, Gujarat, and Maharashtra. Currently, I am the Dean (Research) at RRCE.

Research Focus

My work spans Robotics, Control Systems, AI/ML, and Image Processing. I have authored over 1000 research papers and 20+ textbooks, contributing significantly to the scientific community.
